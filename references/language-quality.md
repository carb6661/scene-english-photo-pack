# Language quality gate

Apply this gate to every candidate word, phrase, and model sentence.

## Selection priorities

1. Scene relevance: connect the item to visible evidence or the scene's ordinary function.
2. Collocational naturalness: prefer chunks native speakers actually combine.
3. Reusability: favour language transferable to common IELTS and CET topics.
4. Productive value: balance recognition vocabulary with language the learner can immediately say or write.
5. Level fit: mostly B1-B2 for Cards 1-4 and B2-C1 for Cards 5-6; never inflate difficulty for appearance.
6. Coverage: enrich the pack with action, interaction, quality, maintenance, access, sustainability, convenience, or atmosphere when supported.

## Register

- Use ASCII `[Speaking]` and `[Writing]` markers in the English strip. Chinese markers rendered with an English font may become tofu boxes.
- Speaking items should sound natural and specific, not like miniature essays.
- Writing items should be precise, defensible, and free of memorised clichés.

## Common repairs

- Prefer `gain / develop communication skills` to `grasp communication skills`.
- Prefer `make a contribution to society` to `make contribution to society`.
- Prefer `the widespread use of the internet` to needlessly ornamental alternatives.
- Prefer precise verbs such as `reduce`, `ease`, `address`, and `discourage` to vague booster phrases.
- Avoid `with the development of society`, `every coin has two sides`, and repeated `more and more`.

## IELTS-oriented checks

- Reward precision and control, not memorised complexity.
- Use `can`, `may`, `tends to`, or `is likely to` when causality is uncertain.
- Include at least one useful evaluation or cause-effect frame across Cards 4-5 when supported.
- For Speaking, add concrete detail or personal relevance.
- For Writing, vary grammar only where the logical relationship is clear.

## Pronunciation

- Default to British IPA for IELTS.
- IPA and spelling must match the taught headword.
- For plural phrases, transcribe the spoken plural.
- Omit uncertain IPA rather than inventing it.

## Final audit

Confirm:

- Card 1 and Card 2 terms match exactly and only Card 1 hides English;
- no unsupported object or action is presented as visibly certain;
- no Chinese translation changes the English claim;
- no duplicated item exists without a pedagogical reason;
- articles, number, prepositions, punctuation, and register are correct;
- no fake quotation, band guarantee, or copied copyrighted passage appears.
